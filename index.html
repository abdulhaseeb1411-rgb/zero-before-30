Why it's different from a budgeting app

Most money in a salary earner's life is not just "spending". It's money lent to a friend, borrowed from a cousin, cash taken out of the bank, a repayment coming back. Z30 treats each of these as what it really is, so you can always see who owes you, who you owe, and what is left of your income.

  • Expenses and income
  • Money you lent, and money you borrowed, tracked person by person
  • Repayments linked to the original loan
  • Transfers between your own accounts, kept out of your spending
  • Fix a mistake or undo an entry; nothing is silently erased

How it works

  1. You write one line. Any mix of English, Roman Urdu or Urdu.
  2. Z30 understands it. If one important detail is missing, it asks one short question.
  3. It's recorded and you can undo it. Your balances and totals come from your recorded entries, not from guesses.

Where it is today

Z30 is in a small private beta. It works in your browser on your phone or computer and can be added to your home screen. English and Roman Urdu work today; Urdu script is still being tested and may make mistakes. We are looking for people who will use it for real and tell us what is wrong.

Your entries are private to your account. No payment details are asked for.
